The books worth reading, and where to read them.

A short, opinionated list of classic computer science and robotics books. No files are hosted here — every entry links to the author's legal free copy when one exists, otherwise to the publisher.

How it works

Links only

The repository stores a single books.json. Nothing copyrighted is hosted; “Read free” always points at a copy the author or publisher has made freely available.

Classics only

A book gets in when it has stayed in print, gone through editions, and is what practitioners actually recommend. Fashionable and new are not disqualifiers, but they are not enough.

Missing something?

Search for it. If it isn't in the list, the search bar looks it up on Open Library, previews whether it clears the bar, and lets you submit it. A GitHub Action re-checks and adds it automatically; borderline cases wait for a maintainer.
